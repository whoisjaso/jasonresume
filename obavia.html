Lesson two

Where your floor leaks.

Six plain questions, one per stage. Answer for your own floor.

  1. Capture. A form fills at 9pm on a Saturday. Is a named person on it by Sunday noon?

  2. Connect. Can your closer read the buyer's own words before the call?

  3. Book. Does every booked call say what it's for?

  4. Discover. Is fit decided from what the buyer said, not from how the call felt?

  5. Agree. Does the closer hear what the setter heard?

  6. Collect. Do you count a deal when the cash is verified, not when they say yes?

Lesson three

Counts yet?

A fictional agency's week, with no amounts. Sort each moment.

  • A call gets booked.

    Not yet. A booking is a promise to talk.

  • The buyer doesn't show.

    Not yet. Nothing happened.

  • The buyer shows up.

    Not yet. A good call is still a call.

  • The buyer says yes on the call.

    Not yet. A yes is not a payment.

  • The first payment is declined.

    Not yet. This is where deals quietly die.

  • The payment clears and is verified.

    This one counts. Cash, verified.

Only one moment counts: paid and verified.

Rank your floor on cash you can see, not on calls you booked.

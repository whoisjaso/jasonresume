Run a sale on it

This is the desk Triple J Auto Investment closes sales on, the one Obavia grows from. Pick a car, scan a license, sign.

  1. Pick the car. From the lot, in one tap.
  2. The odometer. Every document reads this one number.
  3. Scan the license once. Name, address and license number land on every form.
  4. How they're paying. Cash, buy here pay here, or the bank.
  5. The money. Texas sales tax, title, registration and your doc fee.
  6. The paperwork. The bill of sale and the 130-U, filled from the deal.
  7. Signed at the desk. The buyer reads, signs with a finger, done.

The buyer, the cars and the figures in this demo are fictional.

What it does

  • One question per screen. So anyone on your lot can run a sale.
  • Cash, buy here pay here, or bank. Every way your buyers pay.
  • The Texas money, worked out. Sales tax, title, registration and your doc fee, on every deal.
  • The bill of sale and the 130-U. Filled from the deal, in your dealership's name.
  • Rebuilt and salvage titles. Their own disclosures, every time.
  • Ink where ink is required. Those forms print for ink.

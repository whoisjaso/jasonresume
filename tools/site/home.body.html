Plain facts.

Who is Jason Obawemimo?

I founded Obavia, sales software in development for agency owners at $100K to $1M a month, and I co-own and run Triple J Auto Investment in Houston. I build the systems underneath a business, then I run them, from Pearland, Texas.

What is Obavia?

Sales operating software, in development, at obavia.co. It follows a sale from the first inquiry to collected cash in six stages: Capture, Connect, Book, Discover, Agree, Collect. It works inside the agency's own funnel, SOPs and vocabulary.

Can you use Obavia today?

Not yet. Obavia is in development and claims no customers and no results. The waitlist is free and does not create an account or grant access. People and figures in its films are fictional.

What is Triple J Auto Investment?

The Houston used vehicle dealership I co-own and run, at 8774 Almeda Genoa Rd. Cars, trucks and SUVs with in-house financing, sell and trade valuations, and registration support.

What is Handle a Sale?

The sale desk I built for Triple J. It asks one plain question at a time, scans the license once, fills the forms, works out the registration math, picks the right documents, runs in English or Spanish, and keeps every sale on file.

How is Obavia spelled?

O-B-A-V-I-A. Obavia is the company. Obawemimo is the family name.

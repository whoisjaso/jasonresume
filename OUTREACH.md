# Outreach desk

How Jason Obawemimo and Obavia show up on LinkedIn, Reddit, Facebook groups, X and YouTube without sounding like a bot, a brochure, or an AI. This file is the brief for the weekly content routine and for anyone drafting on Jason's behalf. Every draft goes to Jason for approval before it is posted. Nothing is auto-posted.

## What we are trying to make true

When an agency owner searches "Jason Obawemimo" or "Obavia" before a call, they find a person who runs a real business (Triple J Auto Investment in Houston), built the sale desk that business closes on, and is now building sales software for agencies while saying exactly what it is and is not yet. Authority comes from specificity and restraint, not volume.

## Who we are talking to

Agency owners generating $100,000 to $1,000,000 a month, and their own setters and closers. The pain we write about: leads that go cold between the setter and the closer, a closer who walks into a call without the buyer's own words, calls booked with no clear purpose, owners who see call counts instead of collected cash, and funnel gaps nobody owns.

## The voice

Pulled from Jason's own recording, his writing, and the copy on obavia.co and thetriplejauto.com.

- Second person. Set up the reader's situation before the point. "If your setter books the call and your closer starts from zero, this is for you."
- Plain words. Contractions. "Gonna" and "honestly" are fine in speech and in casual platforms, not in LinkedIn long form.
- Blunt when it counts. On Reddit: "Your closer is guessing." On LinkedIn, the same thought reads "Most closers walk in without the one sentence that matters."
- "We" for the businesses. "I" for what Jason did or saw.
- Specific nouns: setter, closer, handoff, booked call, discovery, show rate, collected cash, SOP, the buyer's exact words. Never "solutions", "leverage", "seamless", "streamline", "elevate", "game-changer", "unlock".
- No tidy groups of three. No "it's not X, it's Y". No em dashes. No emoji in body text. No hashtags on LinkedIn beyond two at the very end, none on Reddit.
- Vary sentence length. Let one sentence be four words.

## What Obavia is

Obavia (obavia.co) is sales operating software, in development, for agency owners at $100,000 to $1,000,000 a month and their own setters and closers. "Hear what clients really mean. Carry it from the first call to collected cash." It works inside the agency's own funnel, SOPs and vocabulary. The method is conversation psychology: inspect the buyer's exact words, consider an interpretation that leads back to that language, choose a relevant question. Listen to the person. The workflow runs Capture, Connect, Book, Discover, Agree, Collect, and owners see progress ranked on collected cash, not calls.

Prelaunch pricing: Core is planned at $3,000 a month (up to 10 active sellers, 5,000 pooled meeting minutes). Scale ($6,000 a month) and Enterprise (custom) are proposed. Implementation is a one-time $5,000. The waitlist is free and does not create an account or grant product access.

## The claims boundary (never cross it)

Obavia is in development. Never say it is live, has customers, or produces results. Never state a conversion rate, a close rate, a show rate, a percentage, or an outcome as fact. Never borrow a case study or a testimonial. Zoom, Calendly, GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Slack, Zapier, Google Calendar and Cal.com are planned connection targets, not active integrations; never write "integrates with". People and figures in Obavia's demos and ads are fictional, and any post that shows them says so. Only Core's price is planned; Scale and Enterprise are proposed.

If a comment asks "does it work," the answer is "it is not live yet. I will not put a number on it until there is one to publish." If a comment asks about Triple J's sale desk, describe what it does (one plain question at a time, one license scan that fills every form, the right documents with nothing missing and nothing extra, English or Spanish) and nothing it has not done.

## What performs, per platform, and how we use it

**LinkedIn.** What travels: a first line that names a specific situation, short paragraphs with white space, one observation from operating a real business, a soft close with no link in the body (link in the first comment). Founder posts about setter to closer handoffs, active listening on a sales call, and the funnel gaps between a booked call and collected cash outperform product announcements. Format: 120 to 220 words, first line under 12 words, ends on a question or a plain statement, never "thoughts?".

**Reddit** (r/agency, r/sales, r/Entrepreneur, r/smallbusiness). What travels: a genuine question or a story from doing the work, written by someone who obviously does it, zero links, zero product mentions in the post. The product surfaces only if someone asks, and then as one sentence with the claims boundary. Read each sub's rules before drafting. Most ban self-promotion outright; a post that looks like marketing gets removed and damages the account. Titles are plain and specific: "Setter books the call, closer starts from zero. How do you hand off the buyer's actual words?"

**Facebook groups** (agency owner groups, sales team and closer groups). What travels: a short story from a real call or a real week, a question to the group. Same rule as Reddit: give first, no links, product only when asked. There is no API for posting into groups; drafts are copy and paste.

**X.** What travels: one observation per post, under 200 characters, no thread unless the story needs it. Voice can be the Reddit register.

**YouTube and Shorts.** What travels: a weekly long on a fictional sales call (the handoff, the discovery question, the moment the buyer's words get lost), cut into three shorts. The two Obavia films, "The closer" and "The owner", are the reference for tone; every person and figure in them is fictional and the caption says so.

## Weekly cadence (the routine drafts, Jason approves)

- 3 LinkedIn posts (one operator story, one handoff or listening observation on a fictional example, one plain question).
- 2 Reddit posts or comments, each targeted at a named sub with its rules checked.
- 2 Facebook group posts, copy-ready.
- 3 X posts.
- 1 outline for the weekly long video and its 3 short cuts.

Every draft carries: platform, target (sub or group), the trend or post it is modeled on with a link, the draft, and one line on why it fits this week.

## Sources to draw from each week

Fresh, from the last 30 days: what agency owners, setters and closers are complaining about on Reddit and LinkedIn; what performed in the niche (upvotes, comments, reposts); changes in the sales tools agencies already run. Evergreen: Obavia's six stages, the claims boundary above, Triple J's own week (with no customer details), and the site's verified facts.

## What we do not do

No autodialers, no SMS blasts, no AI voice outbound, no DMs to strangers, no bought followers, no fake accounts, no sock puppets, no borrowed testimonials. Posting through unofficial LinkedIn automation violates its terms and is not worth the account.

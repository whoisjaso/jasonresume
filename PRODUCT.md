# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, founders, operators, and collaborators reviewing Jason Obawemimo's work history, technical range, and contact information. They are likely scanning quickly and need a credible, polished summary they can trust.

## Product Purpose

This project presents Jason's resume and personal profile at jasonobawemimo.com as a public portfolio site and downloadable PDF. Success means the visitor immediately understands Jason's strengths in AI systems, web operations, rental operations, and service-business tooling, then can download or share a professional resume.

## Brand Personality

Composed, capable, direct. The brand should feel polished and serious without becoming cold, decorative, or inflated.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI portfolio tropes, inconsistent colors, cramped resume formatting, noisy decoration, and overly casual resume styling. Avoid designs that look like a template rather than a deliberate professional artifact. Do not publish private phone numbers or a street address on the site or PDF.

## Design Principles

- Lead with credibility: make work history, outcomes, email, and the canonical domain easy to verify.
- Keep the visual system consistent: use the existing ivory, ink, and deep emerald palette without introducing unrelated colors.
- Make scanning effortless: use strong hierarchy, aligned dates, compact bullets, and clear section boundaries.
- Preserve warmth without clutter: the design can feel editorial, but the resume must remain practical and printable.
- Treat the PDF as a primary artifact, not a secondary export.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text. Maintain clear reading order, readable type sizes, sufficient spacing, and a print-safe layout. Avoid relying on color alone to communicate structure. Keep public contact details privacy-safe: email, city/state, and jasonobawemimo.com are acceptable; phone and street address are not.

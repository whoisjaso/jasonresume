What we work on

Three things, in the order they build on each other.

  1. I

    Belief alignmentLine up what you believe with what you want.

    Most people want one thing and believe another. We get clear on three things, in this order.

    1. Your desire. Said plainly, not hinted at.
    2. What you believe about that desire. Whether it's possible, and whether it's for someone like you.
    3. What you believe about yourself. Who you are today, not who you hope to be.

    When what you believe about your desire lines up with what you believe about yourself, you stop chasing it. You are it, now, in the present.

  2. II

    Ego and identityKnow who you are when no one is watching.

    Ego
    How you act when you're perceived, when other people are watching you.
    Identity
    How you act when you're seen only by God, or by no one at all. What you do when no one is looking.

    Ego performs for the room. Identity is what's left when the room is empty. We work on the second one, because it's the one that shows up on the days nobody is clapping.

  3. III

    AI and incomePut AI to work in how you earn.

    I build the CRM, voice AI and automation systems my own dealership runs on, and I mentor people on AI. Here you learn to use it the same way: which tools to reach for, how to think with them, and how to turn what you already know into work AI helps you do.

    No promises about money. You get the skill and the way of thinking. What you build with it is yours.

Fit

Who this is for, and who it isn't.

It's for you if

  • You know what you want and you're tired of not moving on it.
  • You're willing to look honestly at what you believe about yourself.
  • You want AI as a tool you use, not a trend you watch.
  • You'd rather act than post about acting.

It isn't for you if

  • You want a guarantee or a shortcut.
  • You want someone to tell you you're fine as you are.
  • You're looking for a get rich quick plan.
  • You won't do the work between calls.

# Stop Thinking Poor: the video script

For Jason to film himself. The full video runs about four minutes at a calm pace (roughly 600 spoken words). The 30 second cut for Instagram is at the end.

How to read this:

- **[CAMERA]** is framing and delivery.
- **[B-ROLL]** is footage to cut over your voice. Every shot should be real and yours: your desk, your lot, your screen, your street. No stock footage of cars, cash or rented houses.
- Lines in plain text are what you say. Say them in your own words if a line doesn't sound like you. Keep the meaning.

Rules for the take: no money figures, no results, no "you will make", no guarantees, no testimonials. You're teaching a way of thinking and a skill, not selling an outcome.

When it's done: export as `assets/stp/vsl.mp4` (H.264, 1920 by 1080, AAC audio), a still for `assets/stp/vsl-poster.jpg`, and captions as `assets/stp/vsl.vtt`. The page picks all three up on its own.

## The full video (about four minutes)

### 1. The hook (0:00 to 0:20)

**[CAMERA]** Tight on your face, eye level, plain background. No music. Start mid thought, like you were already talking.

Don't post this.

That's the first thing I'm going to ask you. Whatever you take from the next few minutes, don't put it in a caption. Do it.

**[CAMERA]** Beat. Slight lean in.

Because I don't think you're broke. I think you're thinking poor. And those are two different problems.

### 2. The problem (0:20 to 1:00)

**[CAMERA]** Pull out to a medium shot. Relaxed.

Thinking poor isn't about your bank account. It's a belief. It's the quiet voice that says "that's for other people." The one that says you'll start when things calm down. When you have more time, more money, more proof.

**[B-ROLL]** Your hands on the desk. A notebook with a list half crossed out. Your phone face down.

Most people know what they want. The problem is they believe something about that want, and something about themselves, that doesn't match it. So they chase it. And the chasing becomes who they are.

**[CAMERA]** Back to you, direct to lens.

I built Stop Thinking Poor to fix that, in three parts.

### 3. Pillar one: belief alignment (1:00 to 1:45)

**[CAMERA]** Medium shot. Hold up one finger, or don't. Keep it natural.

The first is belief alignment. You get clear on three things, in order.

**[B-ROLL]** You writing three short lines on a page. Close on the pen.

One: what you desire. Said plainly. Not hinted at, not dressed up.

Two: what you believe about that desire. Do you actually believe it's possible? Do you believe it's for someone like you?

Three: what you believe about yourself. Not who you hope to be. Who you are, right now.

**[CAMERA]** Back to lens. Slower.

When what you believe about your desire lines up with what you believe about yourself, you stop chasing it. You are it. Now. In the present. Not someday.

### 4. Pillar two: ego and identity (1:45 to 2:30)

**[CAMERA]** Change angle: three quarters, near a window if you have one.

The second is ego and identity. And this one's simple to say and hard to live.

Ego is how you act when you're being watched. When people see you, when you're perceived.

**[B-ROLL]** A room with people in it, then the same room empty. Your lot after close, lights off.

Identity is how you act when you're seen only by God. Or by nobody at all. What you do when no one is looking.

**[CAMERA]** Back to lens.

Ego performs for the room. Identity is what's left when the room is empty. Most people build the first one. We build the second, because it's the one that shows up on the days nobody is clapping.

### 5. Pillar three: AI and income (2:30 to 3:10)

**[CAMERA]** At your desk, screen visible behind you or beside you.

The third is AI and income. I build the CRM, voice AI and automation systems my own dealership runs on, and I mentor people on AI.

**[B-ROLL]** Your real screen: an editor, a workflow, a prompt you wrote. Blur anything private (customer names, phone numbers, addresses).

I'm not going to tell you AI will make you money. Nobody honest can promise you that. What I'll teach you is how to use it: which tools to reach for, how to think with them, and how to take what you already know and turn it into work AI helps you do. What you build with that is yours.

### 6. Who it's for (3:10 to 3:35)

**[CAMERA]** Medium shot, direct.

This is for you if you know what you want and you're tired of not moving on it. If you're willing to look honestly at what you believe about yourself. If you want AI as a tool you use, not a trend you watch.

It's not for you if you want a guarantee, or a shortcut, or someone to tell you you're fine as you are.

### 7. The offer (3:35 to 3:55)

**[CAMERA]** Tight again. Calm.

Here's how it works. You apply on this page. It's a few questions, and answering them honestly is the first exercise. Then you book a call with me. Thirty minutes. We talk about what you wrote and whether it's the right fit. If it is, I'll walk you through how the mentorship runs and what it costs. There's no price on the page. We talk first.

### 8. The close (3:55 to 4:10)

**[CAMERA]** Hold eye contact. No smile needed. Say the last line slower than everything else.

So don't post about this. Don't save it for later. Scroll down and apply.

No caption. Just action.

**[CAMERA]** Hold two seconds of silence before you cut. Fade to the STP mark on black.

## Before you film

- Read it out loud twice and cross out any line you wouldn't say to a friend.
- Film the hook last, once you're warmed up, then cut it to the front.
- Record clean audio (a lavalier or a phone close to you, in a quiet room). Sound matters more than the camera.
- The page shows "The video is being recorded" until `assets/stp/vsl.mp4` is published, so there's no rush to put up a weak take.

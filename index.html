Read it instead

Still typing the same buyer into every single form? There's a faster way to close a sale. A generic bill of sale off the internet. A 130-U, filled in by hand. Registration math on a calculator. Cash or financed. Insured or not. Salvage or clean. Every answer changes the paperwork. And the whole time, the customer waits. Miss one form, and the deal comes right back to your desk.

So we built a sale desk that already knows the paperwork. Handle a Sale asks one plain question at a time. How are they paying? Who files the registration? Just tap the answer. Scan their license once. Every form fills itself. Nothing typed twice. Priced at $4,000, with registration or without? The math is already done. Every answer routes the deal. The right documents. Nothing missing. Nothing extra. In English, or in Spanish, with one tap.

Your signature is already on them. The customer signs on their phone, or in person. And every sale is saved. If the DMV asks, or the customer calls back, it's all on file. Anyone on your team can close it fast, and right. Triple J. Handle a Sale.

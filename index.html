Overview

  • I designed the lead-to-title data model, roles and validation, and tested Supabase RLS policies to prevent unauthorized role access.
  • I added validation that prevents invalid deal and inventory records from being written.
  • I executed production schema migrations while preserving existing records and operational tables.
  • Its sale desk, Handle a Sale, asks one plain question at a time, scans the buyer's license once so every form fills itself, does the registration math, and routes the deal to the right documents. It works in English or Spanish, and the customer signs on their phone or in person.

Play the sale desk

Sell a car on the desk I built. Fictional buyer, example figures.

  1. Pick the car. From the lot, in one tap.
  2. The odometer. Read back so nobody fat-fingers it.
  3. Scan the license once. Name, address and license number land on every form.
  4. How they're paying. Cash, buy here pay here, or the bank.
  5. The money. Tax, title, registration and the doc fee, worked out live.
  6. The paperwork. Only the forms this deal needs.
  7. Signed at the desk. The buyer reads, signs with a finger, done.

The desk I built

Every sale, start to signed.

Handle a Sale. Triple J closes sales on it. Run it yourself.

I built the desk Triple J Auto Investment closes sales on. One plain question per screen. Scan the buyer's license once and every form fills itself. The money works itself out, and the buyer signs at the desk. Run a deal on it right here.

  1. Pick the car. From the lot, in one tap.
  2. The odometer. Read back so nobody fat-fingers it.
  3. Scan the license once. Name, address and license number land on every form.
  4. How they're paying. Cash, buy here pay here, or the bank.
  5. The money. Tax, title, registration and the doc fee, worked out live.
  6. The paperwork. Only the forms this deal needs.
  7. Signed at the desk. The buyer reads, signs with a finger, done.

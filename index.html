  2. Custom dealership CRM

    Handle a Sale, built for Triple J

    One system: the CRM I built for Triple J, with Handle a Sale as its sale desk. I designed the lead-to-title data model, roles and validation, tested its Supabase row-level security policies against unauthorized role access, and added validation that keeps invalid deal and inventory records from being written. I ran production schema migrations while preserving existing records and operational tables. Three staff use it daily across inventory, deals and follow-up. The sale desk asks one plain question at a time, scans the buyer's license once so every form fills itself, does the registration math, routes the deal to the right documents with nothing missing and nothing extra, works in English or Spanish, and the customer signs on their phone or in person. Every sale is saved on file. TypeScript, React, Next.js, PostgreSQL, Supabase and Vercel, with Edge Functions for the voice and texting webhooks.

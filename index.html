Founding waitlist, Texas pilot
A title packet leaves the dealership looking finished. Three weeks later the county sends it back over one missing signature, and the deal has already gone sideways.The problem Deal Packet Checker exists to remove.

Deal Packet Checker is transaction intelligence for Texas independent dealers. It reads the packet before webDEALER ever sees it, and hands the title clerk a short list of things to look at instead of a stack to re-read.

  • Reads the deal documents and cross-checks them against each other, not against a checklist.
  • Reduces the packet to the exceptions that would actually get it kicked back.
  • Keeps a human in the decision. It flags, the clerk rules.

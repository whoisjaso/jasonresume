# jasonobawemimo.com

Read HANDOFF.md first. It is the brief: what Obavia is, what the site is for, where the work stands, the direction, and the rules that every change has to respect. This file is the short version for a working session.

## What this is

A static site (index.html, tokens.css, game.css, desk.css, intro.js, score.js, hud.js, game.js, game-run.js and game-run.css (loaded on Play), deck.js, verify.js, desk-app.js, track.js, haptics.js; obavia.html shares game.css, desk.css, intro.js, score.js, hud.js and desk-app.js and adds obavia.css and pages.js; the proof pages (credentials, answers, profile, knowledge card, mentions, honor, search) use tokens.css and proof.css) plus Vercel Node functions in `api/`, deployed from `main` to production at jasonobawemimo.com. No build step. No framework. The library, the score, the art, the loops and the tracking are all first-party. HANDOFF.md, section 4, is the current spec; SPEC.md is history.

## Rules that do not bend

- Claims boundary for Obavia: the company and its product share the name. Obavia is a dealership sale desk in development for Texas independent dealers ("Every sale, start to signed"), grown out of Handle a Sale at Triple J. Never say it is live or that dealers use it; never claim customers, results, a conversion rate or a percentage of anything it achieved; Text it to sign, Reach and dealer websites are in development, not live; pricing is not published, so never quote one; people and figures in demos and films are fictional and say so; early access is a conversation, not an account. Source of truth for Obavia is the whoisjaso/obavia repo (obavia-co/index.html and desk/README.md). Apohenia is Obavia's earlier name and appears only as one line in the answers layer. The agency sales product and the hiring page are retired.
- Never publish Jason's private phone or street address. Triple J's business address (8774 Almeda Genoa Rd, Houston) is fine.
- No em dashes anywhere on the site. No gradients on text, no glassmorphism, no three-card rows, no emoji as icons. Fonts are Cormorant Garamond and Hanken Grotesk.
- Second person for the reader's situation, first person for what Jason did, never third person.
- Skips and cancels make no sound and no haptic. Choices, arrivals, sends and unlocks fire sound, haptic and motion in the same frame.
- Every fact on the site must be true and already verified in llms.txt. No invented numbers.
- No model identifiers in commits, PR titles or bodies, or code comments.

## Verify before pushing

`python3 -m http.server 8765` then `node tools/verify/library.mjs` and `node tools/verify/briefing.mjs` (see tools/README.md). Both desktop and mobile. No horizontal overflow, no page errors, no em dashes, every number on the page in llms.txt.

## Where things are

- Home page: the After Hours Library, a console home screen where Jason's career is the game library. Content lives in tools/site/library.json (profile, seats, the six titles, the 18 trophies and the platinum), tools/site/home.body.html (the frame) and tools/site/record.json (the deck and the PDF). `python3 tools/site/assemble_home.py` writes index.html (its JSON-LD comes from schema.json and faq.jsonld). Never hand-edit index.html. The direction contract is the comment at the top of home.body.html.
- Look: game.css (tokens: ink #0A0F0D, lacquer #111B17, bottle #1C3229, bone #EDE7DB, ash #9A9890; no chromatic UI accent, the key art carries the colour; one ease, cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.7, 0.1, 1)). desk.css is the desk as an iPhone app. hud.js owns FX (JG_FX), the toasts (JG_TOAST, JG_NOTIFY), the screen lock, deep links (legacy #story-* links map to the new routes) and keys; game.js is the library engine (focus, open, trophies, seats, routes #title/id, #trophies, #profile, gamepad).
- Player 2: build.js and build.css; every reading, proof and lot problem lives in tools/site/builds.json (each proof a first-person line already in llms.txt). The title screen's build step, the card (drawn to a 1080 by 1350 PNG in the page), the build screen (#build, key J), shared build links (#build/v1/...) and ?for=<role> links. Never add a proof that isn't verified in llms.txt; a thin reading stays thin.
- Art: assets/game/art (one plate per title, graded through tools/art/grade.py), assets/game/portrait, assets/game/medals (tools/art/medals.py). Every shipping raster carries its provenance.
- Onboarding: the title screen. It plays for every visitor on every arrival, on the home page and /obavia.html (only crawlers and clicks between the site's own pages skip it). Press start, then Start (with the score) or Start muted, then "Who's playing?" (interviewer, partner, lurker), then an optional name. Words in tools/site/onboarding.json; intro.js runs it; assemble_home.py writes its head script and data into both pages, so run it after changing onboarding.json. Deep links open after it ends (document event jg:intro-done).
- Sound: score.js plays the adaptive score in assets/score (stems on one clock, a layer per title on the next bar, stings on the beat; nothing loads before Start). tools/score composes and renders it; the sample credits in score.json are printed in the credits.
- Films: assets/game/loops (the living key art, Remotion, tools/film/src/library), and in assets/film the Triple J Handle a Sale film, the Obavia film and the lessons. The Sale Desk film renders from whoisjaso/thetriplejauto, remotion/src/sale-desk.ts.
- The run: After Hours: The Run, a 2D platformer through the record (game-run.js, game-run.css, levels, cast and lines in tools/site/run.json, inlined by assemble_home.py as #run-data). Lazy: game.js loads it on the first Play (key G, the Play button in the bar, the help sheet, "Play the level" on each title, #play and #play/<level>, ?run=<level> which skips the title screen). Someone just looking meets Play right after "Who's playing?"; someone hiring or running a lot keeps their own first step and sees the run as one line on the card. Six levels in record order (Neuroscience, Triple J Auto, Lead to Title, The Inbound, Prospector, Obavia with no medals, in development). Each medal is a trophy from library.json and unlocks that trophy through JG_GAME.unlock; all of them earns the platinum. Fictional characters speak in stock Kokoro voices (`/root/kvenv/bin/python -I tools/voice/narrate_free.py tools/site/run.json` writes assets/voice/run); my own lines are text that play in my voice once tools/voice/run_jason_lines.json is rendered to assets/voice/run/jason-<id>.mp3 and .json (assemble checks the two files agree). Sound and voices only with the score on. Ghosts, boards and the crew count come from api/run.js and stay off without KV_REST_API_URL and KV_REST_API_TOKEN (CONFIG.md) or with Do Not Track or GPC. Never put a fact in a line or a hint that isn't in llms.txt; live game numbers (timer, best times, boards, crew) carry data-run-live.
- Interactive: the desk app in desk-app.js (fictional buyer, example figures), deck.js (resume deck, #present/N), verify.js (Check me). /join redirects to /obavia.html; there is no hiring page.
- Desks and chat: api/guide.js (ask box), api/lead.js (Obavia early access), api/run.js (the run's boards, ghosts and crew), api/_lib. Config in CONFIG.md.
- Analytics: track.js to api/track.js (allowlist there) to PostHog; api/metrics.js and admin.html are the Dailies board.
- Resumes: `python3 tools/site/build_resume.py` writes resume-pdf.html, resume/<id>.html for the nine readings and match.js's data (from builds.json, record.json and lexicon.json); `node tools/verify/resume.mjs` renders and gates the ten PDFs (one page, tagged, no Type 3 fonts, reading order, every number in llms.txt). Never hand-edit resume-pdf.html or resume/. The listing match is match.js (tools/verify/match.test.mjs).
- Copy and facts: tools/site/library.json, tools/site/builds.json, tools/site/record.json, tools/site/home.body.html, api/guide.js, llms.txt, llms-full.txt, answers.json, faq.jsonld. Change a fact in all of them, then assemble and build the resumes.

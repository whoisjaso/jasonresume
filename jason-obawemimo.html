CRM

Triple J's CRM and its Handle a Sale desk

Jason built Triple J's custom dealership CRM (TypeScript, React, Next.js, PostgreSQL, Supabase, Vercel, Edge Functions), and Handle a Sale, the sale desk the dealership closes sales on, is the same system. He designed its lead-to-title data model, roles and validation, tested Supabase row-level security (RLS) policies to prevent unauthorized role access, and executed production schema migrations while preserving existing records and operational tables. Three staff use it daily across inventory, deals and follow-up. Its sale desk asks one plain question at a time, scans the buyer's license once so every form fills itself, does the registration math, and routes the deal to the right documents, in English or Spanish.
